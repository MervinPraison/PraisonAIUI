# Security Policy

We take the security of PraisonAIUI seriously and appreciate responsible
disclosure of vulnerabilities.

## Supported Versions

Security fixes are applied to the latest released version of PraisonAIUI.
Please make sure you are on the most recent release before reporting.

## Reporting a Vulnerability

**Please do not open a public issue for security vulnerabilities.**

Report privately through GitHub's **Private Vulnerability Reporting**:

1. Go to the [Security tab](https://github.com/MervinPraison/PraisonAIUI/security)
   of this repository.
2. Click **"Report a vulnerability"** to open a private advisory.
3. Include as much detail as you can:
   - Affected component(s) and version.
   - Steps to reproduce.
   - Impact assessment.
   - Any suggested fix or mitigation.

If you are unable to use private reporting, you may open a public issue that
contains **no technical specifics** and requests a private channel, and a
maintainer will follow up.

## What to Expect

- We aim to acknowledge new reports within a few business days.
- We will keep you informed as we investigate and work on a fix.
- Once a fix is released, we are happy to credit you in the advisory (unless
  you prefer to remain anonymous).

Thank you for helping keep PraisonAIUI and its users safe.
